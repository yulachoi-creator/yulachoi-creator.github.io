# Yula Choi

### Aftermarket Sales Manager | HD Construction Equipment

I am an **Aftermarket Sales Manager for the Hyundai brand**, working with international dealer networks across **Central America, the Caribbean and Brazil Subsidiary**.

My work sits at the intersection of **sales, dealer management, aftermarket business, and global operations**. I support dealers grow their aftermarket business by managing parts sales, inventory, lubricants, attachments, and various commercial programs while coordinating closely with headquarters and regional organizations.

### What I Do

**Aftermarket Sales**  
Developing sales strategies and supporting dealers to increase parts and aftermarket revenue.

**Dealer Management**  
Building long-term relationships with international dealers and providing solutions tailored to each market.

**Parts & Inventory**  
Managing parts availability, inventory planning, ordering, and supply coordination across global distribution networks.

**Lubricants & Attachments**  
Developing business opportunities for lubricants, breakers, and other construction equipment attachments.

**Global Operations**  
Coordinating with headquarters, overseas subsidiaries, logistics partners, and dealers to resolve complex operational and commercial issues.

### My Approach

I believe successful international business is built on three things:

**Clear communication.  
Practical solutions.  
Long-term relationships.**

Working across different countries, cultures, and business environments has taught me to look beyond individual transactions and focus on creating sustainable value for both customers and the organization.

I am continuously exploring how **data, technology, and digital tools** can make international sales and dealer management more efficient.

> **Connecting people, products, and markets to create sustainable growth.**
